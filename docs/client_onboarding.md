# Client Onboarding Workflow

## Objective

Simulate how a SaaS analytics platform onboards a new enterprise customer.

---

## Step 1: Data Collection

Client provides:

- Customer data
- Order data
- Review data
- Payment data

---

## Step 2: Data Validation

Validation checks:

- Missing values
- Invalid records
- Duplicate entries
- Schema mismatches

---

## Step 3: Data Migration

Validated records are loaded into PostgreSQL.

Data integrity constraints ensure consistency.

Examples:

- Primary keys
- Foreign keys
- Referential integrity

---

## Step 4: Analytics Setup

Business metrics are calculated.

Examples:

- Revenue
- Customer Satisfaction
- Delivery Performance
- Customer Retention

---

## Step 5: Dashboard Deployment

Power BI dashboards are configured for:

- Executives
- Operations teams
- Customer Experience teams

---

## Success Criteria

- Accurate data migration
- Clean datasets
- Reliable KPI calculations
- Actionable business insights
